# 🛍️ Flipkart Ecommerce Analytics | Power BI Dashboard

Hello! I'm Sujata 👩‍💻, and this project focuses on analyzing Flipkart ecommerce sales data using Power BI to identify business trends, understand customer behavior, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To analyze ecommerce sales performance and transform raw data into meaningful insights that help businesses understand sales trends, profitability, customer distribution, and payment preferences.

## 📸 Dashboard Preview

![Flipkart Ecommerce Analytics Dashboard](./flipkart%20dashboard%20image.png) 

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

- **Sales Performance:** Analyze overall sales and business performance.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Understand profit contribution and profitability trends.
- **Customer Analysis:** Explore customer distribution and purchasing patterns.
- **Payment Method Analysis:** Examine the distribution of payment preferences.
- **Time-Based Analysis:** Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Explore data using filters and visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- 🔷 **Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and visualization
- 🧮 **DAX** — Calculations and business metrics
- 🔄 **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- 📊 **Data Visualization** — Interactive charts, KPIs, and slicers

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Sales Performance:** The dashboard reports total sales of 438K across 500 orders, with total profit of 113K.
- **Profitability:** The overall profit margin is 26%, indicating the profitability level shown by the dashboard.
- **Category Analysis:** Electronics leads sales at 0.17M, followed by Clothing at 0.14M and Furniture at 0.13M.
- **Customer Distribution:** Maharashtra (94) and Madhya Pradesh (93) have the highest customer counts among the states displayed.
- **Payment Preferences:** Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most-used payment method, accounting for 45.6% of orders, followed by UPI at 22.07%.
- **Customer Category Mix:** Clothing represents 63.27% of customers, followed by Electronics at 20.53% and Furniture at 16.2%.
- **Monthly Trends:** Sales and profit peak around March, while July shows the lowest values in the monthly chart.
- **Product Profitability:** Bookcases lead the displayed top sub-categories by profit at approximately 16K, followed by Printers and Saree at around 15K each.
- **City-Level Quantity:** Indore (248) and Mumbai (201) have the highest quantity counts among the cities shown.

## 📈 Business Impact & Recommendations

The dashboard can help stakeholders:

- Identify sales trends and high-performing categories.
- Monitor profitability and investigate periods of lower performance.
- Understand customer and payment preferences.
- Make more informed decisions about product strategy, marketing, and business planning.
